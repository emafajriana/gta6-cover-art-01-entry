Sunset Cover Studio

Your photo + a caption = multi-panel cover art in the style of a GTA poster. Offline — photos never leave this machine.

★ Signature: POP-OUT mode — your star breaks out of the panel frame, under a headline in hand-drawn vector lettering — every glyph is code, drawn for this studio as polygon data and filled with liquid chrome. No font files, anywhere.

1 · Photos

Drop photos onto a slot, or pick a file. Click a panel on the cover to edit it. A photo with a transparent background (a cut-out) becomes the pop-out star as itself; any other photo bursts out inside an oval portal.

Burst = how far the star spills past the frame. Load a cut-out (a transparent PNG) as the star and the person bursts out; a plain photo bursts out in an oval portal.
